# CozyMTG

A cozy, single-player Magic: The Gathering collecting game. Travel through Magic's history
era by era, from Alpha in 1993 to the sets of 2026. Play matches against the AI, earn gold,
open booster packs, fill your binder and build decks from what you pulled. It runs offline,
there are no real-money purchases, and it's fine to play in short, relaxed sessions.

## True to the real products

CozyMTG tries to feel like buying Magic at the time each set was in stores:

- **Real pack contents.** Each booster follows its set's real slot layout (the number of
  commons, uncommons and rares, plus land and special slots). It holds the actual printings
  from that set, with their real card images.
- **Products stay in their era.** The shop only sells products that were really on sale in the
  current era. Opponents only use cards that existed at that time.
- **Real rules for each era.** Each era uses the banned and restricted list of its time.
  For example, Era 1 also limits you to one copy of each legend, the 1994 rule.
- **The real look.** The shop shows real photos of the products. Starter decks, booster boxes,
  fat packs and prerelease kits open in 3D.
- **The real rules engine.** Matches run on the [Forge](https://github.com/Card-Forge/forge)
  rules engine and its AI, so cards work like they do at a real table.

## Older products cost more and give more

- **Prices rise over time.** When a product goes out of print, it gets more expensive with each
  new era. Famous early sets also get a collector's premium; for example, Alpha costs four
  times as much.
- **Vintage hits.** Old packs you open later can contain guaranteed "vintage hits". These are
  valuable cards from the set, and the older the pack, the more you get.
- **Artisan cards.** About one pack in six has a card with alternate art that you haven't
  unlocked yet.

## Features

- **32 eras** (Alpha to 2026). Each era has a ladder of opponents in four tiers and a boss.
  Beat the boss to open the next era. The Practice Table always has easy matches.
- **Two side areas:** the **Funhouse** (Unglued, Unhinged and the other joke sets) and the
  **Crossroads** (Universes Beyond: Lord of the Rings, Final Fantasy, Spider-Man and more).
- **The shop:** boosters, booster boxes, fat packs and bundles, prerelease kits, starter decks,
  theme decks, Intro Packs, Duel Decks, Commander precons, Jumpstart, Secret Lair drops, From
  the Vault and other collector's items. You can keep packs sealed and open them later.
- **Commander:** buy Commander precons and play them at 40 life against other precons. Or
  break one up to add its 100 cards to your binder.
- **Sealed events:** open six packs, build a 40-card deck and play three rounds against AI
  players with their own sealed pools. Free events are for practice. Paid events let you keep
  the cards and win packs.
- **Prizes:** FNM, league, Judge and Pro Tour promos for wins, World Championship decks for
  beating bosses, and a set promo with every booster box.
- **Arcade mode** (just for fun, no cards or gold at stake):
  - **Precon duels:** any preconstructed deck against any other.
  - **Commander:** 1v1, or a 4-player free-for-all with three AI opponents.
  - **Cube draft:** pick one of 95 cubes and draft against up to 7 AI drafters, then play three
    rounds.
- **Your binder:** two 3×3 pages per spread, filters, and empty spots for the cards you're
  missing. Unlock alternate arts and sell extra copies for gold.
- **Deck builder:** several decks, legality checks for the era, a mana curve, a color pie and
  Auto-build.
- **A table with atmosphere:** playmats you earn or buy, worn sepia mats for old eras, spell
  effects, and darker rooms for boss fights.

## Playing

The latest stand-alone build is under **Releases**. Unzip it and run `CozyMTG.x86_64` (Linux)
or `CozyMTG.exe` (Windows). You don't need to install anything. Card images download from
Scryfall the first time they're shown.

A new game starts with 550 gold, an Alpha or Beta starter deck, a Revised starter deck and
three Era 1 boosters of your choice. A first deck is built from them for you.
